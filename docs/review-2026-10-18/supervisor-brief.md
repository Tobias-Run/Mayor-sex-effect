I would appreciate a focused methodological review of our English feasibility manuscript. ## Research question, estimand and current evidence

The original question asks whether electing a female mayor affects procurement competition or procedure choice. The proposed close-election contrast concerns electing a particular female-presented candidate instead of her actual opponent. It does not isolate gender from party, experience or other candidate characteristics. Historical public presentation and administrative sex/gender remain different measures.

The acquired bookkeeping frame has **176 election events in 159 municipalities**. It is a selectively acquired inventory across separate cohorts, not a complete national probability sample. Both presentations are supported for 38 pairs, of which 19 are mixed. Nine mixed pairs have original-supported call evidence, six also have tender-count statistics, and four align with the diagnostic first Q1 after the election. **Zero cases are independently certified for a main study.** Older late-term windows are retained separately; the six are not a comparable pooled sample.

Within the current NRW 2025 outcome-linked cohort, the 2-point band contains two female-presented losses and no wins. The 5/10-point bands contain four cases split two/two. A four-column local-linear design has no residual degrees of freedom. Even assuming favorable resolution of all remaining gates, the current count route permits at most ten mixed elections, with a simplified Gaussian 80% MDE of 2.33 outcome SD at alpha .025. This is an assumption-based two-group benchmark, not RDD power, an empirical effect estimate or a universal impossibility bound.

Winner presentation is supported in 52 events, opponent presentation in 39. Documentation/source associations differ by cohort; selection bias is not corrected. Neither complete reporting nor actual procurement decision timing has been verified. More procedures or lots do not create more independent election assignments. Procurement observations retain the **30 September 2026** cutoff.
