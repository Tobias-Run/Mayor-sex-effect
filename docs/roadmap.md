The critical path is **candidate evidence → systematic coverage and unit construction → independent-election precision → go/adapt/stop → preregistration → main research → manuscript/reproduction → Pages**. The [current 55-pair checkpoint](feasibility/nrw-close-election-evidence-review.md) documents six mixed pairs, with two female-presented wins and four losses; the [priority follow-up](feasibility/nrw-close-pair-priority-followup.md) supports 13/15 priority pairs and retains three missing candidate presentations; it exposes measurement and sample limits before further extensive notice parsing. The full 214-pair eligibility ledger precedes a final NRW go decision; prediction labels do not define eligibility.

Planning allowances are approximately **17–33 focused working days to the full feasibility decision**, including an early checkpoint after about **3–6 days**. A successful go decision is followed by approximately **10–18 days for the main specification, analysis and manuscript**, then **2–4 days for Pages**. A conditional expansion starts with a separate **3–5 day audit**, not a promise to finish another state dataset within that period. These are effort estimates; provider delays, missing evidence and scope changes can alter the calendar. No completion date is promised.
