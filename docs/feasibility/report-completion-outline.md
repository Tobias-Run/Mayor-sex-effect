# Completion outline: feasibility and data report

**Working title:** *Can German Municipal Data Support a Female-Mayor Procurement Study? A Bounded Feasibility Audit in NRW and Bavaria.*

The [5 October decision](go-adapt-stop.md) makes this the active research output. **A version-0.1 English [draft](../../manuscript/feasibility-report.md) and [PDF](../../manuscript/feasibility-report.pdf) are available, dated 9 October with the 5 October evidence cutoff.** The sections below are implemented, with a fact ledger, sample-flow and precision figures, 22 public input pins and a [reproduction/verification package](../../manuscript/README.md). FP22 is draft-complete with review pending. This does not complete independent coding or launch an effect analysis. The manuscript prominently attributes the adaptation to Florio and Spagnolo and cites the inspected German foundations with version/access qualifications.

## Report sections and source artifacts

| Section | Required content | Existing evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Question and contribution | Intended candidate-versus-opponent contrast; procurement measurement; feasibility rather than a gender-effect finding | Italian/German literature crosswalk and research protocol |
| Institutions and source scope | State-specific elections, named/anonymous candidates, first-entry versus renewed terms, above-EU procurement reporting and national archive floor | State election/register audits, NRW legal audit and national export note |
| Data and measurement | Frozen snapshot, presentation codebook, exact decisive margins, notice/procedure/lot/result distinctions, no-call dates and missingness | Baseline hashes, codebooks and original phase audits |
| Sample flow | NRW 380 elections → 214 structural pairs → 55 reviewed close pairs → six supported mixed pairs → three provisional candidate-window links; Bavaria 902 general-2020 structural decisions → 95 named → 31 within 10 pp → 24 provisional result-presence municipalities | NRW early matrix and Bavaria band/linkage tables; intersections are explicitly provisional |
| Selection and reliability | Winner/loser evidence availability, search priority, Bavaria's population-based named coverage, unknown dispositions and independent-review status | Selection audit, candidate register and independent-coding protocol |
| Precision and design | Municipal election is the independent assignment; exact margin support; Gaussian planning assumptions and conditional ceilings; no actual RDD power or effect estimate | Early benchmarks, design-support table and conditional pooled scenarios |
| Decision and limits | Current causal launch is no-go; no null-effect conclusion; source-specific generalizability; reopening conditions | Go/adapt/stop memorandum |
| Reproduction | Input manifests, checksums, software environment, tests, local-versus-public artifacts and future source-version failures | Source/register manifests and executable audit scripts |

The [sample-flow figure](../../manuscript/figures/sample-flow.svg) and [precision plot](../../manuscript/figures/precision-benchmark.svg) are generated from the existing public tables. They label hypothetical SD assumptions, the cutoff scale, provisional versus confirmed events and the absence of effect estimates. Result units remain separate from independent elections; indexed missingness and query absence do not mean no original outcome or no purchasing.
