The completion tranche stops at sixteen sources; its explicitly documented combined ceiling is 1.125 GiB. Total new successful archive bytes are **87,451,766**, and tracked procurement bytes across the project are **1,114,683,423**. These figures measure successfully retained archives, not total network transfer. Failed and over-budget requests are not absence evidence.

Eleven statistics have calendar-mature nine-month follow-up targets, versus two before recovery. Calendar maturity does not certify result coverage. Overlapping result revisions and unsupported chronology remain withheld; ongoing `open-nw` results are not zero-bid outcomes.

## Additional provisional mixed-count election

**Bückeburg, Niedersachsen 2021**, has an absolute finalist gap of **6.8902 pp**, ten supported original 2025 call procedures and five supported tender-count statistics. Two historical primary party sources explicitly describe the opposing finalists as a female mayoral candidate and a male mayoral candidate in 2021. They support a **first-coder mixed pair with a female-presented loss**. Official state-source identities and votes were verified in the earlier acquisition phase; those returns are headed provisional.

The [assignment checkpoint](original-recovery-assignment-checkpoint.json) therefore adds **one provisional mixed-count election**, bringing the inventory to **five across separate cohorts**: four NRW 2025 elections (two female-presented wins/two losses) and one Niedersachsen 2021 election (one loss). These are not five comparable observations in a pooled causal sample. Niedersachsen's late-term full-year 2025 calls differ from NRW's Q1 2026 calls and immature follow-up. The new case lies outside the 5 pp acquisition stratum; 10 pp remains an acquisition band, not an adopted RDD analysis bandwidth.

The [presentation source manifest](original-recovery-presentation-source-manifest.json) retains two accepted originals and a failed CDU brochure request (404). The dated SPD block is 18 March 2021; the CDU district article is 24 August 2021. Two search batches per finalist were used, with three original-page requests including the failed PDF. The first search batch preceded the protocol pin; subsequent original acquisition followed it. Person annotations and source bundles stay local. Labels describe public presentation, not administrative sex. **Independent second coding is uncompleted; zero main-study elections are certified.**

This targeted candidate review improves one linked lead; it cannot measure frame-wide documentation bias. The next candidate-review batch must include unmatched elections and losing finalists, rather than condition all review on supported outcomes. Exact legal entry, uninterrupted exposure, result revisions and complete follow-up remain open. Many procedures do not replace independent elections, and five provisional cases do not establish adequate precision.

## Decision and next work

Continue bounded feasibility acquisition. Prioritize domestic identifiers for the 303 calls without GUIDs and unresolved originals, while retaining all 114 elections and negative source outcomes. Broaden historical candidate review across matched and unmatched cases under a separate pinned batch rule. Seek independent coding only through an authorized reviewer workflow; no contacts have been made. Reassess precision separately by cohort once assignments, exposure and coverage are certified. No causal estimator or outcome rate is launched. The historical report and earlier acquisition artifacts remain frozen; GitHub Pages remains deferred until research completion.

## Reproduction

With the pinned private caches available, run `python src/pilot/original_recovery.py --bueckeburg-completion` and `python src/pilot/recovery_assignment.py` from the repository root. Both commands replay locally without new acquisition. The initial tranche is separately replayable using the first command without its completion flag; it writes the initial recovered public outputs, so rerun the completion command to restore the latest checkpoint. `python -m unittest discover -s tests` passes 208 tests. Latest artifacts reproduce deterministically; 23 earlier acquisition artifacts, the baseline pins and all 32 frozen report input/output pins are preserved. Raw originals are not redistributed, so full replay requires the local handoff/source bundle rather than a fresh public checkout alone.
