# Expanded-frame original audit checkpoint

11 October 2026. The first original-validation pass after the authorized band/window expansion **supports 47 observed tender-count statistics across 16 municipal elections**. Four of those elections lie in the newly opened >5–10 pp stratum. These are procurement observations with candidate assignment still to be reviewed; they do not add four verified mixed elections or establish a causal sample.

## Calls first, originals before outcomes

The complete capped TED context requests start from the 167 procedure IDs already present in the 497 selected call records. Four cohort-specific queries return 320 standard competition/result notices published by the unchanged September cutoff. There is no lower query-date cutoff, allowing an older documented competition root to disqualify a later correction rather than masquerade as a new call. No result availability or candidate presentation determines the GUID query roster.

The audit rechecks the 231 retained German daily-export snapshots and finds **171 exact UUID/version/GUID originals**, with no additional original archive downloads. The other **149 indexed originals remain missing from this cache pass**. Seventy-eight procedures in 31 municipalities support a documented original call graph, actual sole municipal contracting-party roles, and original issue/publication dates inside the cohort's fixed window. This is not a finding of complete original or outcome coverage.

Thirty-one municipalities with supported original-window calls and sixteen with observed supported counts are subsets of the unchanged 114-election roster. All unmatched and unresolved elections stay in the [linked inventory](expanded-original-linked-election-inventory.csv). The 303 domestic call records without procedure IDs remain a separate unresolved discovery queue; title similarity, buyer names or the number of notices do not manufacture GUIDs or independent purchases.

## What the count support means

An observed supported statistic requires an original result, an explicit valid total-tender statistic, successful reciprocal lot/tender/contract links, municipal buyer scope, a stable supported called procedure category, and contract chronology after original call publication. Result issue/publication must fit the original call's nine-calendar-month target and the administrative cutoff. Overlapping result lots remain withheld for revision review. Grouped lots and cross-lot contracts retain their source units; they are not extra elections.

Calendar maturity does not establish complete result search, full result revision coverage or legal exposure eligibility. The previous four provisional NRW mixed-count elections remain under their original audit rules; they have not been recertified under a new follow-up design.

Continue original acquisition and independent assignment measurement; do not estimate effects yet. Prioritize the 149 missing exact originals and the 303 domestic records, preserving the full 114-election denominator. For original acquisitions, reuse exact caches and set a new bounded date queue before downloads. Review historical presentation and losing-finalist evidence independently of result matches, plus legal entry/turnover and jurisdiction. The four newly opened count-bearing elections are a practical validation queue, not an outcome-selected causal sample. Reassess precision on supported independent mixed elections after these checks.
