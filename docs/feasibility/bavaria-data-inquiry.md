# Draft provider inquiry: Bavarian historical mayoral elections

Status: preparation only; not sent. Intended recipient is the Bavarian State Statistical Office's relevant election-data team, using an official contact route once confirmed. No recipient address is assumed.

## Research purpose

We are assessing a project adapting Florio and Spagnolo's *Female Mayors and Public Procurement* to Germany. The proposed design compares close decisive mayoral contests between a woman and a man and links municipalities and actual terms to observed procurement outcomes. Data feasibility is not yet established.

## Questions to resolve

1. Are candidate-level mayoral results for 2014, 2020 and off-cycle elections available as machine-readable historical data? Which earlier and later years are covered?
2. Are all candidates, source-recorded gender, exact votes, valid ballots, nominations, municipality identifiers, first-round/runoff dates and final winner included?
3. Are actual term starts, reelections, early exits, acting mayors and successor dates recorded separately from first entry into office?
4. Does the current officeholder workbook have archived snapshots, and can its nomination-level votes be linked to named candidates in historical files?
5. How are municipal mergers, identifier changes, write-in candidates, uncontested elections and corrected results represented?
6. What application, affiliation, fee, license, storage and disclosure requirements apply? Is linking to procurement records allowed under those conditions?
7. Are data dictionaries, completeness notes and example extracts available for assessing a pilot?

Do not attach the entire research repository or candidate data unnecessarily. Confirm institutional applicant information before any actual application. A German-language version can be prepared for the provider if needed; project documentation remains English.
