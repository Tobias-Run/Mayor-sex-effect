# Bounded decision: end the current causal claim, complete a feasibility report

**Decision date: 5 October 2026. Scope: the present public-source NRW-2020 study and the tested named Bavaria-2020 expansion route.** Following the supervisor's feedback, the early precision gate and the single bounded expansion test are complete. **Do not launch or preregister the proposed confirmatory causal study from this inventory. End its current causal claim and complete a reproducible feasibility/data report.** A future causal study can be reopened only on material new linked-sample evidence; this is not a finding that female mayors have no effect or that German causal research is universally impossible.

The report will prominently explain that this is a German feasibility assessment for adapting Florio and Spagnolo (2026), [*Female Mayors and Public Procurement*](https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.7046899). The [German research crosswalk](literature-design-crosswalk.md) includes Schild (2013), Baskaran and Hessami (2018, Hesse female mayors; 2025, Bavarian councillors), Arnold, Frank–Stadelmann–Torgler and GERDA. The intended causal contrast was electing a specific female-presented candidate instead of her actual opponent. It never isolated gender holding the rest of the candidate fixed.

The observed failure is joint measurement/linkage/support, rather than a chosen universal minimum sample size. The substantive minimum effect and empirical variance are still unspecified; the illustrative 5/10/20-pp grids do not set relevance. This decision does not claim a completed outcome-specific RDD power analysis. A positive go finding would need that analysis, valid support and independent coding. A bounded no-go can occur before spending the full collection budget to construct them.

## Why not expand collection now?

The tested TED route is useful for a data report, but no source has yet demonstrated scalable historical candidate measurement **and** usable comparable outcomes. Hand-searching all unnamed Bavaria municipalities or all remaining NRW cases has no evidence-based expected mixed-pair yield. More notices in the same municipalities cannot fix assignment counts. Predicted gender, current officeholder sex, wider numerical bands and a new unvalidated panel design are not replacements for the failed gates.

The broad statewide queue remains a future lead: 45 structural Bavaria general-2020 decisions fall within 2 pp, compared with six currently named. The twelve-election combined ceiling applies only to the tested named route, not to all conceivable public or provider-held data. Within 10 pp, the current named inventory permits an extremely optimistic combined ceiling of 76; this excludes known NRW same-presentation pairs and assumes every other unknown resolves favorably. Neither figure is a linked sample, a collection forecast or justification for choosing a bandwidth.

Reopening therefore needs a **materially different bulk route**, not repeated low-yield searches. A named provider delivery or public release could justify another bounded audit if its access, losing-candidate fields, dates, provenance and procurement join are inspectable. No provider or author has been contacted. The [prepared Bavaria inquiry](bavaria-data-inquiry.md) remains unsent. Hesse's older public replication record remains a methods/access lead; it has not been audited as a contemporary expansion sample.

## Complete the research under the feasible scope

The active deliverable becomes a **feasibility/data report** about whether available German election and procurement sources support the Italian adaptation. Use the [report completion outline](report-completion-outline.md) to build it from existing evidence. Report sample flow, units, registry limitations, reporting/date coverage, selection and precision assumptions. Do not calculate an under-supported effect or interpret inability to estimate as a null result.

Keep the two nominated outcomes—negotiated-procedure share and conditional single-tender share—as measurement questions. Do not report them as effects or as comparable municipal rates until their denominators pass. Strategic criteria, values and winners remain optional measurement extensions, not a larger confirmatory family.

The first-coder/source audit is reportable now. Independent review remains outstanding: the 28 prepared NRW packets are not completed double coding. The report must either incorporate a separate reviewer's actual dispositions or explicitly retain the limitation. No invented reviewer, repeated first-coder work or mechanical checksum agreement will be counted as independence. Unavailable pretreatment covariates, exclusions and source-access failures remain visible.

This checkpoint closes the bounded launch decision. It does **not** mark final RDD precision, full attrition construction or independent review complete. Broad acquisition and main-study packages remain on hold. The original 17–33-day causal feasibility allowance is not activated. Report writing and reproduction use the remaining decision-block allowance; any additional acquisition or reopened study needs a concrete new route and budget.

GitHub Pages stays deferred until the report is complete. Its eventual feasible scope would explain coverage, provenance, sample loss and assumption-based precision interactively. It must not display invented causal estimates or suggest the Italian results have been replicated. The existing website release gate remains in force.

## Conditions for reopening the causal study

A new decision must document all of the following, without tuning choices to effect estimates:

1. Additional **independent historical elections**, with both actual finalists identified and consistently measured, enough observations on both cutoff sides, and independent coding.
2. Outcome-specific buyer/beneficiary linkage, comparable original competition dates, follow-up, reporting denominators, exposure/turnover and deduplicated units.
3. A justified substantive effect threshold and design-specific useful precision, with small-sample/dependence limitations and institutional comparability if states or cohorts are pooled.
4. Explicit selection/attrition evidence and a bounded expansion budget before main acquisition and preregistration.

The retained artifacts make a future reassessment possible. The conclusion for the available evidence is a completed feasibility finding and a no-go for the present causal launch.
