## Precision comes before more extensive case collection

For the **four NRW 2025 count-linked provisional elections**, the existing optimistic balanced Gaussian pooled-t benchmark requires an effect of approximately **8.01 outcome standard deviations** for 80% power at per-test alpha .025. This is not RDD power; final certification could reduce the sample. Each older newly linked cohort currently has only one female-presented loss, so it does not have both cutoff sides and cannot supply its own comparison.

Under the already documented illustrative balanced Gaussian model, **42 elections** are needed for 1.0 SD, **156** for 0.5 SD and **612** for 0.25 SD at alpha .025/80% power. These are planning scenarios, not adopted substantive thresholds or RDD requirements. Even the 66 municipalities with current positive CSV count metadata are an optimistic source opportunity ceiling for that specific discovery snapshot, not 66 mixed elections. Additional months/originals may enlarge it; not all rows have linked calls, eligible exposure or verified assignments. Do not pool the six cases to present a spurious precision gain.

This is a source-scope opportunity, not full identity/assignment/exposure certification.
