# Acquisition-band and observation-window revision

Decision authorized by the project owner on 11 October 2026; this document is pinned before new procurement discovery. It supersedes the acquisition ceiling for this new phase while preserving all earlier ledgers and the frozen historical report. It is an internal prospective acquisition rule, not an external preregistration.

## Decision and tradeoffs

Increase the **absolute final-vote gap between the two finalists** from 5 to **10 percentage points** for acquisition. In a complete two-candidate round a 10 pp candidate gap corresponds to the winner receiving 55%, i.e. 5 pp above 50%. Preserve cumulative 2/5/10 pp counts and mutually exclusive ≤2, >2–5, >5–10 strata. Select regardless of candidate names, predicted presentation, winner or procurement matches. Keep missing names and unresolved elections. Do not reinterpret the previous 5 pp checkpoint as a 10 pp study.

The wider band increases independent-election opportunities, but elections further from a tie can be less comparable; bias and functional-form sensitivity can grow. Ten pp is **not** the adopted causal RDD bandwidth. A later design must justify its running variable, bandwidth, density/balance checks, clustering, precision and narrower sensitivity analyses. Do not select bandwidths on effect significance. More lots are not more independent elections; repeated municipalities remain shared clusters.

Screen the same pinned GERDA source across 2018–2025 and extend the active cohort roster to **all ≤10 pp NRW 2025, Niedersachsen 2021, and Brandenburg 2024/2025 municipal decisive pairs**. NRW selection uses its official statewide register; verify new NI pairs against the existing state PDFs and new BB pairs against the official municipal-result pages. County/association offices, ties and quality flags remain excluded. Provisional state returns remain distinguished from final legal certification.

## Longer window and comparable follow-up

For the older active cohorts, **Brandenburg 2024 and Niedersachsen 2021**, acquire standard procurement calls from **1 January–31 December 2025** (twelve complete calendar months). This is the first full calendar year after the BB election and a separate late-term window after the NI election. It is not automatically a first twelve-month period after legal entry, and the two cohorts remain separate. Extending beyond Q1 can convert more elections into linked cases, but also changes seasonality, exposure length, turnover risks and the measurement population. No outcome-driven window selection or automatic pooling.

For **NRW 2025 and Brandenburg 2025**, retain the existing January–March 2026 discovery window in this execution. Their full 2026 calendar year is not yet observed, so do not compare a partial year as if complete. Any later full-year extension must retain every selected election and the same follow-up rule.

The new longer-window analysis targets **nine calendar months of result follow-up after each verified original call publication**, with an administrative source cutoff of **30 September 2026**. Calendar addition clips to the destination month's final day. This makes all 2025 calls mature by the cutoff once their original dates are verified; 2026 Q1 calls require follow-up into October–December 2026 and remain immature under this rule. Do not shorten their follow-up or drop immature/missing-result cases. Earlier Q1 analyses retain their original cutoff and are not silently overwritten. Acquire calls first, verify original roots/corrections and both issue/publication dates against the call window, then apply outcome/scope/exposure rules. Metadata discoveries do not establish roots, purchases or tender counts.

Do not relax actual municipal buyer roles, country/type checks, original-call/result linkage, treatment evidence, legal chronology or independent second coding. Datable candidate and party self-presentations are already admissible under the existing codebook; names, occupations and generic office titles do not become person-level assignment.

## Sources, resource bounds and reporting

Reuse the six pinned national monthly CSVs for Q1 2025/2026, all existing original caches, and both NI PDFs. Acquire only the nine missing monthly CSVs April–December 2025 in chronological order, at most 32 MiB compressed per archive, at most 192 MiB new procurement bytes, and at most 1 GiB combined procurement bytes including the earlier 757,474,906-byte NRW bundle and 117,362,156-byte Brandenburg/national bundle. Stop and report incomplete coverage before exceeding any cap. At most three new BB official HTMLs, 5 MiB each. No new XML download in this discovery phase; original auditing remains a separate work queue. No contacts or Pages deployment.

Report nested election counts, new-vs-prior events, distinct municipalities, name availability, official-source status, exact municipal standard-call metadata counts, missing procedure IDs, source coverage, right censoring and the unchanged number of supported mixed-count elections. Never replace missing source coverage with zero procurement, merge correction notices into purchases without evidence, or infer a certified sample from a larger acquisition frame. Reassess cohort-specific linked-election precision before main-study launch.
