Checked on **5 October 2026** under the adopted [candidate and exposure codebook, version 1](candidate-exposure-codebook.md). This project adapts **Florio and Spagnolo (2026)** to Germany and builds on the [German literature](../literature.md). After the [priority evidence follow-up](nrw-close-pair-priority-followup.md), historical public presentation is supported for **40 of 110 candidates**: **six mixed pairs, ten same-presentation pairs and 39 unresolved pairs**. ## Measurement and review procedure

Administrative sex/gender remains unresolved for every candidate. The supported measure here is **documented public gender presentation explicitly linked to the 2020 candidacy, election or activity**. A named Frau/Herr, feminine personal role or unambiguous personal pronoun can qualify; names, photographs, family descriptions, generic masculine office labels and GERDA predictions cannot. A current contact title beside an older role-start date is not automatically a historical presentation statement.

The universe was selected from **all 214 official two-person decisions**, without filtering on predicted gender. The 15 within 2 pp were searched first; all other 40 received initial searches afterwards, with additional source follow-ups. Inclusive bands use exact candidate-to-candidate vote differences, not rounded displayed shares or the distance above 50%. Procurement outcomes were not used to select candidate evidence. These bands prioritize acquisition and do not choose an estimation bandwidth.

Every finalist keeps its original official name and an independent local review disposition. Manual identity corroboration is recorded for an abbreviated given name in Iserlohn, compound given names in Horn-Bad Meinberg and Übach-Palenberg, and a directly addressed surname/council-group role in Ratingen. The duplicated given-name field in Rösrath and the Leittersdorf/Leitterstorf discrepancy in Sankt Augustin remain unresolved; no silent name repair supplies an assignment.

## Original evidence and rejected leads

The [source manifest](nrw-close-pair-source-manifest.csv) records **180 source pins/routes**. It preserves the initial 123 routes (55 official vote files, 64 additional routes and four reused cached originals) and adds **57 priority follow-up routes**. Of these, **168 originals** are cached and checksum-verified; **12 routes** remain unavailable. The follow-up adds 53 acquired originals and four failed attempts without rewriting the initial source records. Dates, authorship, retrieval precision, bytes and failed-attempt reasons are separate. Some live pages are undated or retrospective; a dated archive path does not prove unchanged historical content.

The [search log](nrw-close-pair-search-log.csv) records the 55 initial query strings and maps them to recorded responses. The [response inventory](nrw-close-pair-search-responses.csv) pins all 31 discovery-response batches, including supplemental searches. Supplemental query strings were not fully captured in this public inventory, so it is not presented as a complete independently replayable search history. File-record timestamps are labeled as such, not as query execution times. Search snippets never supply assignments. The separate [priority query log](nrw-close-pair-priority-search-log.csv) now captures all **60 exact follow-up queries** and maps them to **15 complete request/response artifacts** in the [follow-up inventory](nrw-close-pair-priority-search-responses.csv); timestamp semantics are explicit. This complete follow-up log does not repair the earlier supplemental-query gaps.

Material review decisions include:

- The Beckum 2020 utility accounts are scanned. Both named Herr presentations on **physical PDF page 22, printed page 18**, were OCR-extracted and visually checked. Operating-director dates on this page are not mayoral term boundaries.
- A personal pronoun in an Iserlohn candidate biography refers to the candidate's father. It was rejected; the accepted evidence is a separate 2020 party nomination original. A Leichlingen retrospective has a pronoun referring to another municipality's participant and was rejected. The second finalist is now independently supported by a direct named address in the official 9 November 2020 minute.
- The Monschau school page reproduces an **Eifeler Zeitung** article. Its institutional host does not satisfy primary authorship, despite clear feminine wording. Two party links failed and a municipal report exceeded the 32 MiB download cap.
- Four initial downloads were mistakenly stopped by a broad client keyword filter matching CAPTCHA code for comment forms or footer assets. Ordinary responses from the **same URLs** were inspected; no access challenge was solved or bypassed. The corrected source pins preserve this history. The Horn-Bad Meinberg and Sendenhorst articles supplied qualifying named relative pronouns. Generic masculine titles and family descriptions were not used.
- The party-authored Viersen letter is dated **7 May 2020** inside the **22 September 2020** official meeting pack; the official host and pack date do not replace authorship or the letter date. The follow-up independently strengthens that finalist with an official-authored 1 September 2020 council minute; the other finalist remains unresolved. The Frechen letter date, 23 September, is likewise distinct from its 30 September posting.

## Reproduction and remaining work

For the cached original snapshot and reconstructed local manual worksheet, run from the repository root:

```bash
python src/pilot/nrw_close_pair_sources.py --all-election-originals --ocr-beckum
python src/pilot/nrw_close_pair_screen.py --write-public-tables
python -m unittest discover -s tests
```

Collection/construction uses Python's standard library, Poppler and Tesseract (`eng`). The source script verifies cached hashes, downloads missing successful pins when requested, preserves failed routes and rejects changed mutable originals. The statewide acquisition option reads the existing 382-pin election manifest directly, without requiring GERDA data. It no longer treats the presence of generic CAPTCHA assets as an access challenge. OCR rerendering uses 120 dpi and refuses to overwrite a changed reviewed derivative. Exact HTML extraction honors declared source charsets where recorded, preserving Latin-1 accented names. The follow-up Viersen calendar JSON requires an ordinary public page session and is discovery-only; its dynamic response is not guaranteed to reproduce through a bare GET. Accepted labels rest on the separately pinned original documents.

Classification also requires the local manual worksheet at `data/interim/nrw-close-pair-screen/reviews.json`. A fresh checkout can reproduce original votes and acquire the pinned accessible sources; presentation labels must be independently reconstructed by inspecting those originals under the codebook. The worksheet has two finalists in official source order, accepted label/tier/cue, primary-authorship and person-reference checks, 2020 context and date precision, exact locator, identity corroboration and OCR visual-review flags. The public event table is a review guide, not a substitute for that manual evidence step. Code checks worksheet completeness, original hashes, exact locators, historical linkage and identity status before producing signed mixed-pair margins.

**Validation:** all 380 official vote sources were reparsed, all 168 available close-review pins matched, and **132 offline tests passed**. The reviewed Beckum OCR was regenerated identically in the initial audit. The new official pages in Leichlingen, Übach-Palenberg and Viersen were visually inspected as well as text-checked.
