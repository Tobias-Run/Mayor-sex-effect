## An independent call-first roster exposes missing results

Starting only from published results can omit ongoing, cancelled or unreported procedures. A separate capped search therefore selected **all 30 official pairs within 5 pp**, without a presentation or result-availability filter, and queried competitive calls published in Q1 2026.

It returned 165 indexed calls. Exact municipal buyer-name/local-authority checks provisionally match **46 notices to 41 procedure GUIDs in 15 municipalities**. Follow-up to 30 September returns 78 context notices (51 competitions and 27 standard results); **14 of the 41 procedures have no indexed standard result by the cutoff**. They remain unresolved/censored, not zero-tender or cancelled observations. This roster does not capture procurement without a call and therefore does not establish the complete procedure-choice population.

Reusing the retained exports already recovers 50 original XMLs for this independent roster. Twenty-five original competition graphs are supported; **23 procedures have both original issue and publication in Q1**. Sixteen graphs remain pending, with 28 original notice targets still missing from these retained export dates. Two supported roots fall outside the candidate Q1 issue/publication window. Counts from this new roster are separate from the fully recovered 46-result batch.

Königswinter has one provisional call and no indexed result, despite lacking a Q1 procedure in the earlier result-first list. Mülheim has six provisional Q1 calls and no indexed standard results for those GUIDs; two original Q1 roots are already verified. Availability from the result-first list alone is therefore insufficient to select eligible municipalities. We keep all 30 municipalities in the [call roster matrix](nrw-2025-q1-call-municipalities.csv), including zero exact matches with unknown coverage.
