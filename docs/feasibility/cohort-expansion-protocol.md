# Bounded independent-election expansion protocol

Recorded 11 October 2026, before the new Brandenburg procurement queries. This is an acquisition protocol, not a registered analysis plan or an adopted RDD bandwidth. The project adapts Florio and Spagnolo (2026); [Italian and German research](../literature.md) informs the institutional/design review.

## Fixed frame and first acquisition batch

Use the already pinned GERDA mayoral-candidate snapshot at commit `176259ea551b12a56dcd0c15b168e0e6e608f59a` (candidate CSV SHA-256 `7ab9425dc9b4b1625eadcd5ff001689cec72e32d5f278a5740a29c7b1e9212b1`) to compare 2023–2025 state/year cohorts for complete decisive two-candidate rounds, names, quality flags and vote reconciliation. This initial source-availability comparison has already been inspected; no new procurement outcomes inform the selection below. GERDA is a research compilation, not a nationwide census. Keep missing names and all presentation states visible in the availability table; predicted labels do not certify treatment.

The first extension is **all ten Brandenburg 2024/2025 structurally screened pairs within 5 percentage points**, regardless of gender predictions, winner side or procurement availability: Kremmen, Schöneiche bei Berlin, Stahnsdorf, Angermünde and Lychen (2024); Bad Freienwalde (Oder), Glienicke/Nordbahn, Velten, Teltow and Kolkwitz (2025). None duplicates an NRW municipality. Brandenburg offers full named candidates in this source, unlike the inspected Bavaria, Saxony-Anhalt and Thuringia recent losing-candidate rows. Other states remain candidates for later acquisition; this is a bounded starting batch, not a declaration that their elections are absent.

Validate each selected decisive pair against original official results: municipality, round/day, both full identities, exact votes, valid-vote denominator and declared winner. Record source discrepancies without silently replacing them. The five-pp rule retains the preceding acquisition band; it does not widen a causal bandwidth after seeing results. All ten remain in the acquisition ledger even if a source or match is missing. Primary-source verification and later coding are separate from eligibility.

## Comparable windows and role checks

For the **2025** elections use original competition issue AND publication in **1 January–31 March 2026**, indexed result follow-up through **30 September 2026**, matching the candidate NRW calendar window. For the **2024** elections use the analogous **Q1 2025** and **30 September 2025** follow-up cutoff. Preserve these as separate cohort strata: election months differ and Q1 is not a common number of months since office entry. Exact office chronology, municipal powers, procurement regimes, geographic vintages and any pooling justification remain open. Do not present the extension as a pooled certified sample.

Query all selected municipalities in each cohort for standard called competitions before collecting results. Recover correction roots and exact notice UUID/version/procedure identifiers. Zero exact municipal matches means unknown coverage, not zero procurement. Missing or late results stay in the frame; do not select municipalities on completed awards. Apply the new reciprocal lot/tender/contract graph audit, retaining multi-lot contracts without duplicating contracts or elections. Called-procedure composition is a restricted diagnostic; the nominated all-category negotiated share remains unsupported until no-call procurement is acquired under a harmonized entry rule.

Use the established public-presentation evidence standard for BOTH finalists, including same-prediction pairs. No inferred labels from names, photos, generic office titles, or future roles. Cap the first coding search at two targeted query rounds and four candidate-specific originals per finalist; unresolved cases stay unresolved. Independent second coding is unfinished and must be completed before a main-study decision. Election ITT assignment and continuous original-winner exposure remain distinct.

## Resource bound and stopping decisions

Each year gets a maximum 1,500 indexed call notices and 1,500 procedure-context notices, preserving complete pagination or reporting a failed capped query. Reuse source-pinned German exports first. The first original-XML acquisition batch is bounded to **64 additional daily exports and 256 MiB additional compressed bytes**, with **1 GiB combined retained Q1-expansion export bytes** including the previous 757,474,906-byte NRW bundle. Read each network response only up to its remaining budget and verify ZIP structure and source hashes. No retrospective broad harvesting or contacts are included. Public manifests/code/aggregate ledgers may be uploaded; original documents and person annotations remain local. GitHub Pages remains deferred.

Stop this batch at its fixed ten-election frame, evidence-search cap or export budget. Recompute independent-election support by cohort, observed candidate label status and result availability, keeping excluded/unresolved rows and both winner sides. Repeated elections in one municipality in future batches must be flagged and clustered; they cannot automatically count as independent assignments. Report conditional precision without replacing missing results by zero.

The prior planning scenarios (0.25, 0.5 and 1.0 outcome SD; 80% power; two-sided per-test alpha .025) remain **illustrative**, with balanced Gaussian benchmarks 612, 156 and 42 elections. They are not substantive effect thresholds or RDD requirements. This first ten-election extension cannot by itself meet even 42 independent linked mixed elections when added to the existing four. The next decision must consider whether a fixed broader source frame can plausibly supply useful design-specific precision BEFORE substantial further collection. If it cannot, stop the causal main-study route and retain descriptive/feasibility research. No causal estimator, null-effect claim or preregistered threshold is authorized by this protocol.
