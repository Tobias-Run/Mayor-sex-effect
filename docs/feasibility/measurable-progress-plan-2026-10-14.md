# Measurable progress toward a causal main study

Planning checkpoint: 14 October 2026 UTC. This is a prospective work plan, not a new acquisition or eligibility result. It supersedes open-ended case chasing as the operational next step; earlier audits and the historical report remain unchanged.

## Objective and baseline

Test whether a defensible, informative causal study can be built around electing a particular female-presented finalist rather than her actual opponent. We adapt the Italian study described in [the literature review](../literature.md), with the German research and institutional differences explicitly retained. The estimand is not an isolated gender effect.

Success means either more independently eligible elections under a defensible common design, or a documented closure of a route that cannot deliver them. Counts of files, downloads, tests, lots and notices are not primary progress measures. No sample gain is promised.

## P1: One scoreboard, with meaningful denominators

Track each event through separate gates: source frame; official identities/votes and decisive legal rule; both historical candidate measures; original municipal buyer and procedure evidence; common observation window and reporting coverage; office/exposure and intention-to-treat definition; independent coding; final eligibility. Record pass, fail or unresolved with a source and reason at every gate. A failed gate is a completed review, not an eligible case.

Report each cohort separately and retain nested acquisition bands at 2, 5 and 10 percentage points. These bands do not determine the eventual RDD bandwidth. Deduplicate municipalities before quoting independent-cluster counts; retain repeat elections with explicit dependence flags. Missing outcomes are not zero outcomes, and eligibility cannot be conditioned on observing a post-election award or a successful contract.

Every batch receipt records: events attempted, completed dispositions, newly supported mixed pairs, newly original-supported links, newly independently eligible cases, female-presented wins/losses, request count and outstanding reasons. Report the change from the preceding checkpoint, including zero gains. Compare source/assignment completion for winners and losers and for matched and unmatched municipalities.

## P2: Seven Saxony 2022 events, not selected success stories

Review the entire screened 2022 cohort: Oelsnitz/Erzgeb., Roßwein, Elsterberg, Königsbrück, Reichenbach/O.L., Neustadt in Sachsen and Dresden. This is the complete 2022 subset of the existing close-plurality screen, not a claim to cover all Saxon mayoral elections.

Reconcile official valid votes, every finalist, ranking and winner. Verify the historical legal provision applicable to each event; a current general plurality rule is insufficient. The top-two running margin uses all valid decisive-round votes. Record runner-up/third-place separation and assess whether a two-contender causal comparison is defensible before combining any observations with two-person runoffs. Do not adopt a third-candidate cutoff after inspecting outcome estimates.

Use person-specific historical primary evidence for both top contenders under the unchanged [candidate codebook](candidate-exposure-codebook.md). Names or photos do not resolve presentation. Official identity/vote and presentation checking precedes procurement prioritization; outcome absence does not remove an event from the audit.

Initial cap: 30 new source retrieval attempts for this seven-event batch, with at most two attempts per exact URL. Reuse verified caches. Before starting, pin the event order and request ledger; stop at the cap and record unresolved reasons. No paid access, access-control bypass or new bulk archive tranche. A further batch requires a dated rationale based on the first batch's yield.

## P3: Test procedure choice before replacing an outcome

Retain all 55 NRW 2020 elections in the historical availability denominator. The five current mixed-presentation method leads are Velbert, Rösrath, Iserlohn, Wilnsdorf and Unna. Select five additional, disjoint calibration controls from the same frame: two supported same-presentation pairs, two unresolved pairs with method metadata, and one without a pre/post method match. Select within each stratum by SHA256 of `ags + "|procedure-calibration-2026-10-14"`, ascending. If a stratum is too small, publish its shortfall and pin an amendment before inspecting new originals; never silently substitute a convenient successful case.

This ten-case sample calibrates source validity and selection mechanisms; it is not an effect-estimation sample. For every case, reconcile transformed method details to originals, municipal buyer scope, unique procedure identifiers, pre/post timing, called and no-call procedures, missing records and repeat publications. Capture mismatches rather than silently correcting them. The broad method field can label competitive negotiation as `open`; literal detailed categories therefore need source validation. Bid subcounts cannot stand in for total bids.

Deliver a proposed single primary measure with explicit numerator, denominator, decision-date rule, observable source population and common pre/post windows. First-document date is not automatically the procurement decision date. An all-procedure negotiated share fails calibration if no-call procedures or their timing cannot be measured comparably. A narrower called-procedure measure would change the target population and requires a dated [outcome codebook](outcome-population-codebook.md) amendment, explicit selection assessment and re-evaluation of the Italian adaptation. The two previously nominated outcome candidates are not silently replaced. A failed prototype is a valid deliverable.

Initial cap: 30 new retrieval attempts for ten calibration cases, at most two per exact URL, using cached bridge records first. Pin the case list and expected source checks before acquisition. Do not treat the five existing method leads as five additional supported count elections.

## P4: Independent review and election-relative eligibility

The existing [readiness record](current-cohort-independent-coding-readiness.json) covers 13 pairs / 26 blank forms, including all six provisional links and seven exclusions or unknowns. Sources, person-level annotations and the decoding key remain private. Keep reviewer forms free of first-coder labels, winner/margin information and outcome availability. Review exclusions as well as proposed inclusions; retain disagreements before adjudication.

For each linked pair, audit office entry, turnover and procurement decision timing, and define election-relative observation windows with comparable reporting cutoffs. Decide the intention-to-treat estimand before any outcome-dependent retention. Calendar-mature follow-up does not prove complete reporting. The four NRW 2025 cases and the two older cases cannot be summed into a comparable causal sample without a justified design.

Completion metrics: completed independent pairs / assigned pairs, candidate-level agreement, disagreements resolved / disagreements observed, coverage dispositions, and eligible distinct municipal clusters by cohort. Until completed reviewer records exist, independent completion remains zero.

## P5: Precision before another expansion wave

After P2 and P3, produce a feasibility decision even if P4 is still pending. Show (a) currently supported cases, (b) independently eligible cases, and (c) explicitly hypothetical upper bounds if every remaining unknown were resolved favorably. Upper bounds must still respect observable outcome availability, compatible windows and municipal dependence; do not sum incompatible cohorts.

Evaluate substantively meaningful standardized effect scenarios of 0.25, 0.5 and 1 SD, alongside interpretable outcome units once a measure is defensible. Use actual treatment balance, distinct clusters, running-variable support, bandwidth sensitivity and plausible missingness in a design-aware simulation when enough information exists. If these inputs cannot be estimated credibly, report that limit and conservative bounds rather than a fabricated power estimate. Never use exploratory causal estimates to choose the outcome or expansion queue.

The [existing Gaussian scenarios](creative-precision-scenarios.csv) are optimistic planning benchmarks: without predictive adjustment, the three scenarios require 612, 156 and 42 balanced observations at alpha .025 and 80% power. Assumed R-squared .75 lowers those benchmarks to 156, 42 and 14; no such predictive performance is established. These are neither RDD sample requirements nor launch thresholds. Validate any predictor with pre-election data and held-out municipalities; no post-treatment predictors or fitted in-sample R-squared claims. A final one-primary-outcome testing rule may change the benchmark and must be declared separately.

## Decision rules and stopping discipline

**Prepare the main study** only when the primary population/outcome, legal assignment and common windows are defensible; independent review and exposure/coverage gates pass; and precision for a substantively relevant effect is credible under the proposed causal design. A round number such as ten or twenty cases is not a go criterion.

**Expand selectively** only when a completed route demonstrates actual joint-linkage yield and a named complete state/year frame has a defensible upper bound capable of addressing the precision shortfall. Before acquisition, pin the frame, selection-independent ordering, source/request cap, expected conversion calculation and decision criterion. High notice volume alone is insufficient. Historical outcome adjustment and multi-candidate elections remain separate routes until their validity is established.

**End or redesign the causal claim** if both prototypes fail validity or their defensible sample bounds remain uninformative and no named expansion route can close the gap. Preserve the audited dataset and publish a feasibility result or explicitly descriptive analysis. This is a research finding, not a failed delivery.

The next milestone is the P2/P3 decision memorandum, not unlimited collection: seven Saxony event dispositions, ten historical calibration dispositions, the complete 55-election availability denominator, an updated eligibility scoreboard and a precision/expansion decision. At most one additional explicitly amended retrieval batch per prototype may precede that decision; unresolved cases stay unresolved. No causal launch occurs while independent coding is incomplete.
