# Research pipeline

Current election pilots use Python's standard library. The [Bavaria reproduction guide](../docs/feasibility/bavaria-feasibility.md#reproduction) documents pinned acquisition, structural screening and an independent historical-workbook audit. Outputs and source data remain local and ignored by Git.

The [operational Bavaria pilot](../docs/feasibility/bavaria-operational-pilot.md) adds pinned official-report extraction and a historical TED JSON acquisition and municipal buyer-linkage script. It preserves notice, lot and contract fields separately and excludes ongoing competitions from awarded tender-count outcomes. Python's standard library and Poppler's `pdftotext` are sufficient; the source-linkage tests run without downloads.

The historical register preserves nominations and anonymous candidate slots. Candidate names are recovered only from complete exact vote-vector matches; gender and actual terms are not inferred. Broader procurement cleaning and the main analysis remain conditional on measurement, coverage and precision checks.

The [candidate and tenure supplement](../docs/feasibility/bavaria-candidate-and-tenure-register.md) documents `bavaria_official_title_evidence.py`, `bavaria_candidate_register.py` and `bavaria_legacy_ted_awards.py`. They retain dated presentations, actual initial appointments, source-bounded intervals and scoped legacy award units separately. Downloads must match pinned hashes; mutable HTML is reviewed rather than automatically accepted. The offline tests guard period selection, half-open intervals, repeated supplier blocks and multi-lot value reconciliation.

The [statewide NRW register](../docs/feasibility/nrw-statewide-election-register.md) documents `nrw_election_register.py`. It expands acquisition beyond the earlier 102 district runoffs, retains explicit no-election municipalities, excludes county offices and checks 380 detail files against published source hashes. It handles single-candidate ballots separately, preserves full candidate identities and keeps predicted gender labels and first-observed election dates out of actual treatment/tenure assignment.

The [NRW operational pilot](../docs/feasibility/nrw-operational-pilot.md) adds `nrw_ted_linkage.py`, `nrw_legacy_ted_awards.py` and `nrw_official_evidence.py`. It keeps municipal department/beneficiary aliases in a scope-review queue, distinguishes cancelled result notices from awards, checks named historical person roles and reports temporal overlap separately from responsibility. Shared verified-source helpers accept a separate raw-data root while preserving existing Bavaria defaults. All 31 offline tests pass.

The [expanded NRW supplement](../docs/feasibility/nrw-expanded-pilot.md) documents `nrw_buyer_scope.py`, `nrw_full_ted_awards.py` and `nrw_candidate_followup.py`. It applies individual full-notice buyer/beneficiary decisions, parses legacy and eForm award sections with distinct typed counts and dates, flags nominal one-euro values, and retains party-authored historical presentation separately from municipal sources. The source-pinned pipelines run successfully; the combined suite passes 39 offline tests.

The [latest NRW follow-up](../docs/feasibility/nrw-pilot-completion.md) extends `nrw_full_ted_awards.py` for explicitly grouped legacy lots and offer ranges without a winning price. `nrw_primary_presentation_update.py` adds exact archived official candidate presentation and separate autobiographical entry/dated official activity evidence. It never treats deputy-record ends as mayoral term ends. The current combined suite passes 45 offline tests; the full-notice pipeline yields 52 units with dates and total counts across three elections.

The [complete NRW cohort](../docs/feasibility/nrw-complete-pilot.md) runs `nrw_full_ted_awards.py` with an explicit 96-source manifest, raw root, output path and `--require-complete-cohort`. All 92 retained notices are audited; 106 observed award units have dates and total tender counts. It separates unfinished competitions, winner versus unsuccessful-bid fields, one-cent values, conflicting scalar money and undefined source lots. `nrw_term_law.py` verifies pinned historical statutes and checks the council calendar without assigning individual office entry or procurement authority. The current combined suite passes **58 offline tests**; earlier test counts above describe their respective stages.

The [six-municipality extension](../docs/feasibility/nrw-extension.md) adds `nrw_extended_ted.py`, `nrw_extension_evidence.py` and `nrw_municipal_registers.py`. They verify two complete indexed TED pages, preserve 51 pending scope cases, link five new dated/current primary observations to official finalists, and audit three city-specific tenure CSVs. Year precision, honorary deputy roles, vacancies, academic titles, blank ends and a stale Münster current-role entry remain explicit. The current combined suite passes **68 offline tests**. The new 133 retained indexed notices supply no verified total tender counts; blocked full-text acquisition does not change the original cohort's 106 dated/count award units.
