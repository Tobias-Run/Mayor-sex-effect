# Research pipeline

The new [early feasibility audit](../docs/feasibility/early-feasibility-decision.md) runs `nrw_early_feasibility.py` on pinned existing artifacts, before further acquisition. It generates 55-election linkage, descriptive selection and hypothetical-variance Gaussian precision benchmarks without estimating treatment effects. Actual-margin matrix ranks expose inadequate cutoff-side support. Numerical dependencies are pinned in [analysis/requirements-feasibility.txt](../analysis/requirements-feasibility.txt); the main RDD stack remains unselected. `--prepare-independent-review` creates 28 local pair packets, suppressing previous labels/votes and storing the first-coder key in a separate directory. Preparation is not a completed independent review. Six tests check the benchmark against distributional references/raw Monte Carlo and protect insufficient-side/unknown-label behavior; the combined suite passes **138 offline tests**. Older stage counts below retain their historical scope.

Current election pilots use Python's standard library. The [Bavaria reproduction guide](../docs/feasibility/bavaria-feasibility.md#reproduction) documents pinned acquisition, structural screening and an independent historical-workbook audit. Outputs and source data remain local and ignored by Git.

The [operational Bavaria pilot](../docs/feasibility/bavaria-operational-pilot.md) adds pinned official-report extraction and a historical TED JSON acquisition and municipal buyer-linkage script. It preserves notice, lot and contract fields separately and excludes ongoing competitions from awarded tender-count outcomes. Python's standard library and Poppler's `pdftotext` are sufficient; the source-linkage tests run without downloads.

The historical register preserves nominations and anonymous candidate slots. Candidate names are recovered only from complete exact vote-vector matches; gender and actual terms are not inferred. Broader procurement cleaning and the main analysis remain conditional on measurement, coverage and precision checks.

The [candidate and tenure supplement](../docs/feasibility/bavaria-candidate-and-tenure-register.md) documents `bavaria_official_title_evidence.py`, `bavaria_candidate_register.py` and `bavaria_legacy_ted_awards.py`. They retain dated presentations, actual initial appointments, source-bounded intervals and scoped legacy award units separately. Downloads must match pinned hashes; mutable HTML is reviewed rather than automatically accepted. The offline tests guard period selection, half-open intervals, repeated supplier blocks and multi-lot value reconciliation.

The [statewide NRW register](../docs/feasibility/nrw-statewide-election-register.md) documents `nrw_election_register.py`. It expands acquisition beyond the earlier 102 district runoffs, retains explicit no-election municipalities, excludes county offices and checks 380 detail files against published source hashes. It handles single-candidate ballots separately, preserves full candidate identities and keeps predicted gender labels and first-observed election dates out of actual treatment/tenure assignment.

The [NRW operational pilot](../docs/feasibility/nrw-operational-pilot.md) adds `nrw_ted_linkage.py`, `nrw_legacy_ted_awards.py` and `nrw_official_evidence.py`. It keeps municipal department/beneficiary aliases in a scope-review queue, distinguishes cancelled result notices from awards, checks named historical person roles and reports temporal overlap separately from responsibility. Shared verified-source helpers accept a separate raw-data root while preserving existing Bavaria defaults. All 31 offline tests pass.

The [expanded NRW supplement](../docs/feasibility/nrw-expanded-pilot.md) documents `nrw_buyer_scope.py`, `nrw_full_ted_awards.py` and `nrw_candidate_followup.py`. It applies individual full-notice buyer/beneficiary decisions, parses legacy and eForm award sections with distinct typed counts and dates, flags nominal one-euro values, and retains party-authored historical presentation separately from municipal sources. The source-pinned pipelines run successfully; the combined suite passes 39 offline tests.

The [latest NRW follow-up](../docs/feasibility/nrw-pilot-completion.md) extends `nrw_full_ted_awards.py` for explicitly grouped legacy lots and offer ranges without a winning price. `nrw_primary_presentation_update.py` adds exact archived official candidate presentation and separate autobiographical entry/dated official activity evidence. It never treats deputy-record ends as mayoral term ends. The current combined suite passes 45 offline tests; the full-notice pipeline yields 52 units with dates and total counts across three elections.

The [complete NRW cohort](../docs/feasibility/nrw-complete-pilot.md) runs `nrw_full_ted_awards.py` with an explicit 96-source manifest, raw root, output path and `--require-complete-cohort`. All 92 retained notices are audited; 106 observed award units have dates and total tender counts. It separates unfinished competitions, winner versus unsuccessful-bid fields, one-cent values, conflicting scalar money and undefined source lots. `nrw_term_law.py` verifies pinned historical statutes and checks the council calendar without assigning individual office entry or procurement authority. The current combined suite passes **58 offline tests**; earlier test counts above describe their respective stages.

The [six-municipality extension](../docs/feasibility/nrw-extension.md) adds `nrw_extended_ted.py`, `nrw_extension_evidence.py` and `nrw_municipal_registers.py`. They verify two complete indexed TED pages, preserve 51 pending scope cases, link five new dated/current primary observations to official finalists, and audit three city-specific tenure CSVs. Year precision, honorary deputy roles, vacancies, academic titles, blank ends and a stale Münster current-role entry remain explicit. The current combined suite passes **68 offline tests**. The new 133 retained indexed notices supply no verified total tender counts; blocked full-text acquisition does not change the original cohort's 106 dated/count award units.

The latest [procedure and historical-presentation audit](../docs/feasibility/nrw-procedure-and-presentation-audit.md) adds `nrw_procedure_audit.py` and `nrw_historical_presentation_followup.py`. A complete 225-notice enrichment and two previous-competition context rows are pinned separately. Procedure GUIDs and exact notice versions identify a Viersen result history, while reused local/contract references stay unmerged. A permutation-safe single-lot rule identifies four total tender counts whose dates remain missing; unequal flattened arrays are never zipped. Four further party-source observations preserve letter dates, named event references, historical month precision and uncertain publication-day labels. The current combined suite passes **80 offline tests**; earlier counts above describe their respective stages. Full-text outcome coverage remains 106 dated/count units across three elections.

The [nationwide structured-export audit](../docs/feasibility/national-procurement-open-data.md) adds `federal_procurement.py` and `nrw_federal_procurement.py`. Twenty-eight pinned monthly archives cover all 57 known result UUID/version pairs. Original XML preserves scoped lot/result/tender/contract references, typed total counts and distinct date meanings; converted CSV buyer/statistic disagreements remain explicit. Thirty supported XML pairs include eighteen existing PDF matches, eleven new Viersen results and one additional grouped Geilenkirchen result. The combined observed inventory is 118 paired units across four elections, before complete deduplication. Fifty separately pinned original competition notices provide phase context for 38 GUIDs and stay outside the retained result inventory. The current combined suite passes **96 offline tests**. Main treatment assignment and GitHub Pages remain deferred.

The subsequent [buyer and phase audit](../docs/feasibility/nrw-buyer-and-phase-audit.md) adds `nrw_buyer_followup.py` and `nrw_phase_linkage.py`. Sixteen original-XML decisions add 12 city result notices and five dated/count observations; 37 scope cases remain pending. The combined inventory has 237 result notices and 123 paired observations across six elections. Fifty-nine original competition XML notices, 63 literal legacy-PDF previous references and two original result-XML references establish supported phase chronology for 115 observations. Correction graphs require city/GUID scope, exact parent versions, chronological edges and one acyclic documented root; unlinked roots remain missing. Earlier acquisition metadata lacking notice versions is supplemented from the separately pinned enriched index. `--write-public-tables` regenerates the reviewed derived ledgers. The current combined suite passes **110 offline tests**. Actual treatment, complete deduplication and adequate RDD precision remain unestablished.

The [procedure-specific timing and term follow-up](../docs/feasibility/nrw-procedure-scope-and-term-followup.md) adds `nrw_procedure_scope.py`. It checks 69 original result XMLs and 63 original legacy PDFs, providing source procedure types for all 123 paired observations. Three no-call cases require separate timing rules; five competitive-procedure timing cases remain under review. Two exact-title Geilenkirchen candidates have different GUIDs and stay unmerged. The pipeline checks primary Italian table captions and sample labels against a pinned official PDF, retains year-level history and oath events without filling 2020 office entry, and preserves official Werdohl nomination dates/names/occupational wording separately from registry gender. Optional downloads must match the primary/index pins; archived legacy PDFs remain a dependency. All **118 offline tests** pass. Earlier test counts above describe their respective stages.

The [historical close-pair review](../docs/feasibility/nrw-close-election-evidence-review.md) adds `nrw_close_pair_sources.py` and `nrw_close_pair_screen.py`. They acquire pinned originals without a GERDA dependency, reparse all 380 official elections, and select all 55 decisions within an exact inclusive 10 pp margin. The source-pinned local manual worksheet provides two independent finalist dispositions and validates authorship, historical context, identity exceptions and exact locators. All 55 initial reviews are recorded. After the [priority follow-up](../docs/feasibility/nrw-close-pair-priority-followup.md), six mixed and ten same-presentation pairs are supported and 39 remain unresolved; 27/30 priority candidate presentations are supported. Follow-up rows retain a separate completion status. Exact HTML locator checks honor declared legacy charsets recorded in the source pins. Current contacts, generic masculine titles and predictions never supply a signed margin. Scanned Beckum evidence has separately reviewed OCR/visual provenance. All **132 offline tests** pass; raw sources and person-level annotations remain local, with event-level evidence tables and manifests published.
